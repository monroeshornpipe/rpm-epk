The Sound

Untamed acoustic chaos, refined.

Formed in 2018, Rabid Possum Monster has worn many faces — but the sound never changes: unbridled, untamed, unlike any other acoustic act on the Gulf Coast. From blistering bluegrass anthems to trippy space grooves and everywhere in between, RPM delivers unhinged, thrilling live shows.

Call it acoustadelic — part psychedelic, part swamp, part bluegrass, all heart. The trio is house band at the legendary Red Bar in Grayton Beach, Florida.
